Questions

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. The van carries its own water tank and generator, so we can work in a driveway, a parking lot or on the street.

Do I need to be home?

Not for exterior work. For interior work we need the car unlocked or the keys left with someone on site.

What if it rains?

We can still detail your car in the rain. If you'd rather wait for a dry day, it's your call. Just let us know and we'll move your appointment.

Can you detail apartment or office parking lots?

Usually, yes. Some buildings don't allow washing on site, so check with your property manager. Interior-only details are almost always fine.

How do I pay?

Cash, Venmo or Zelle, after the job is done.
