Our Process

Every detail is done by hand, one step at a time, with professional products and no shortcuts. Here's what goes into it.

Exterior

  1. Wheels and tires first. Wheels, barrels, calipers, and tires are cleaned with dedicated brushes and cleaners so brake dust never touches the paint.
  2. Foam pre-wash. The whole car is covered in thick foam that loosens dirt and grit before anything touches the paint.
  3. Hand wash. A careful hand wash with clean wash mitts, panel by panel, to avoid swirls and scratches.
  4. Door jambs and trim. The areas most washes skip get cleaned too.
  5. Dry and dress. A soft drying towel and blower get water out of every gap, then tires are dressed and glass is left streak-free.

Interior

  1. Air blowout. Compressed air pushes dust out of vents, seams, and crevices before vacuuming.
  2. Deep vacuum. Seats, carpets, mats, and trunk, finished with clean carpet lines you can see.
  3. Steam and wipe-down. Dash, console, cupholders, and door panels are cleaned and sanitized.
  4. Leather and fabric care. Leather is cleaned and conditioned; fabric seats and carpets can be shampooed and extracted.
  5. Glass and final touches. Windows inside and out, then a last look at every surface.

Made for Your Car

No two cars come to us in the same condition. A weekly driver needs something different from a family SUV after a beach trip or a weekend exotic. We look the car over first and adjust the process to what it needs, from pet hair and sand to stains, odors, and paint that needs correcting.

How Ceramic Coating Works

What It Does

  • Deep, glassy gloss that lasts far longer than wax or sealant
  • Water beads and sheets off, taking dirt with it
  • Resists water spots, bird droppings, road salt, and UV fading
  • Makes every wash faster and easier

A coating is not armor. It won't stop rock chips or deep scratches; that's what paint protection film is for.

What Happens on Coating Day

  1. Wash and decontaminate. Foam pre-wash, hand wash, iron remover, and clay bar so the paint is completely clean.
  2. Correct the paint. With a 1-step or 2-step package, we machine-polish out swirls and light scratches first. The coating locks in whatever is underneath, so this is where the finish is made.
  3. Prep and coat. We wipe every panel with a panel prep to remove oils, then apply the coating one section at a time.
  4. Inspect. We go over the car under light to make sure there are no high spots before handing back the keys.

Aftercare

  • Keep the car dry for the first 24 hours and wait about a week before the first wash while the coating fully cures.
  • Hand wash with a pH-neutral soap. Skip automatic car washes with brushes.
  • Dry with a clean microfiber towel or a blower.
  • Get a maintenance wash every few weeks to keep the coating performing. A Fresh Start is ideal.

Questions

What are your hours?

Monday through Saturday, 8am to 7pm. Closed Sunday. We work rain or shine; if you'd rather wait for a dry day, we'll reschedule.

What areas do you serve?

All five NYC boroughs, plus Nassau and Suffolk counties on Long Island. Not sure if you're in range? Put your ZIP code in the booking form and we'll let you know.

Do I need to provide water or electricity?

No. The van carries its own water tank and generator, so we can work in a driveway, in a parking lot, or on the street.

Do I need to be home?

Not for exterior work. For interior work, we need the car unlocked or the keys left with someone on site.

What if it rains?

We can still detail your car in the rain. If you'd rather wait for a dry day, it's your call. Just let us know and we'll move your appointment.

Can you detail at apartment or office parking lots?

Usually, yes. Some buildings don't allow washing on site, so check with your property manager. Interior-only details are almost always fine.

What products do you use?

Only professional-grade products from brands detailers trust: Koch Chemie, CarPro, Superior Products, Gyeon, Adam's Polishes, Bio-Bombs, and Armour Detail Supply, with microfiber towels from The Rag Company. Ceramic coatings are from Gyeon and Adam's Polishes.

What if I'm not happy with the result?

Before we leave, we walk around the car with you and look it over together. If anything isn't right, point it out and we'll fix it on the spot.

How do I pay?

Cash, Venmo, or Zelle, after the job is done.
