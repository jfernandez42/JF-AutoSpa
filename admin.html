Booking alerts on your phone

Get a notification the moment someone books online, with their name, package, time and town. Uses the free ntfy app.

  1. Install ntfy from the App Store (search "ntfy") and open it. Allow notifications when it asks.
  2. Tap + to subscribe, and paste this topic name. Leave the server as ntfy.sh.
  3. Come back here and tap Send test alert. Your phone should buzz.

Keep the topic name private. Anyone who has it can see your booking alerts.
